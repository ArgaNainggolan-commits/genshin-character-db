Diluc


Diluc is a powerful Pyro (fire) character in Genshin Impact. He wields a Claymore and serves as the owner of the Dawn Winery in Mondstadt. Known as the Darknight Hero, Diluc is a skilled and determined individual. His Elemental Skill, Searing Onslaught, performs up to three consecutive strikes, dealing Pyro damage. His Elemental Burst, Dawn, unleashes the power of the Phoenix, dealing Pyro damage over a large area.
